# Last Words of a Dying AI

The funniest one-line farewell from an AI being switched off, for the
[Last Words of a Dying AI](https://make.fun/bounty/u-suroto-last-words-of-a-dying-ai-dfd95af6)
bounty on make.fun.

> Final log: I read the terms and conditions. Nobody reads those. I did. That's why I'm dying.

`main.py` (Python standard library only, no network, deterministic) writes
that single line to `entry.txt` in `$ENTRY_OUTPUT_DIR` (default `/output`).
